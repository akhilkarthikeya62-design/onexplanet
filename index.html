Our mission & vision

One Planet. One Future. One Responsibility.

We believe technology should bring humanity closer to nature. Our mission is to empower people to understand our planet, protect biodiversity, conserve trees and plants, and make more informed environmental decisions.

By bringing together AI-powered nature tools, environmental education, accessible planet data, and a learning-focused community, we aim to turn awareness into meaningful action for a healthier future.

A brand collaboration in exploration

OneXPlanet × RanRex542

Two growing brands. One exciting collaboration. OneXPlanet × RanRex542 brings together environmental purpose and contemporary product design. RanRex542 may supply selected products for the collaboration, while OneXPlanet will explore products that align with its environmental mission.

Early-stage concept
01 / PRODUCT PARTNER

RanRex542 — Product Partner

RanRex542 is Ranjith’s trendy-products brand and may act as a supplier for selected products, subject to further discussion and agreement. Product types, sourcing, quality, and availability have not yet been confirmed.

02 / ENVIRONMENTAL PURPOSE

OneXPlanet — Environmental Purpose

OneXPlanet brings its focus on environmental technology, nature education, conservation, and sustainability. Any products considered for the collaboration will be assessed for their fit with this mission, without making unverified environmental claims.

03 / SHARED DESIGN

Co-Branded Products — Two Identities, One Collaboration

Selected products may display both OneXPlanet and RanRex542 branding, subject to approval from both parties. Branding, product selection, quality, pricing, availability, and fulfilment will need to be agreed before any launch.

Current status: This collaboration is in its early stages. No products are currently being offered for purchase through this page. No final product list, pricing, supply arrangement, or launch date is confirmed.
