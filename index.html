Our mission

One planet. One future. One responsibility.

We believe technology should bring humanity closer to nature. Our mission is to empower people to understand our planet, protect biodiversity, conserve trees and plants, and make more informed environmental decisions.

By bringing together AI-powered nature tools, environmental education, accessible planet data, and a global community, we aim to turn awareness into meaningful action for a healthier future.

Meet the founder

Akhil Karthikeya

Founder & CEO, OneXPlanet

Akhil Karthikeya is an aspiring young innovator who believes curiosity and technology can inspire people to care more deeply for the natural world. Through OneXPlanet, he is building toward a vision where environmental learning, thoughtful AI, and community action help make protecting our planet more accessible to everyone.

“The future of our planet is shaped by the choices we make today. Let’s use knowledge, innovation, and compassion to protect nature for generations to come.”
